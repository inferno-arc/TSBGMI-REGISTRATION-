📜 TSBGMI Rules

  • 🚫 No Cheats: Hacks, ESP, aimbot, scripts, mods or unauthorized third-party tools are strictly prohibited.
  • 🎮 Registered Players Only: Only players submitted during registration may participate.
  • 👥 No Teaming: Teaming or intentionally cooperating with another team during a match is prohibited.
  • 🐛 No Bug/Glitch Abuse: Exploiting game bugs or glitches for an unfair advantage is not allowed.
  • 🔐 Room ID & Password: The match Room ID and Password will be provided 15 minutes before the scheduled match through the official tournament communication channel.
  • 📱 Check Official Channel: Captains should regularly check the official WhatsApp group/channel for announcements.
  • ⏰ Join On Time: Players should join the room before the scheduled match start time.
  • 🔒 Don't Share Room Details: Room ID and Password should not be unnecessarily shared outside participating players.
  • 🔄 No Unauthorized Substitution: Registered players cannot be replaced without organizer permission.
  • 📸 Evidence for Disputes: Players may be asked to provide screenshots or video evidence when reporting an issue.
  • 🏆 Scoring: Results will follow the official scoring system announced by TSBGMI.
  • 🚫 No Unfair Exploitation: Players cannot intentionally abuse technical issues or game mechanics for an unfair advantage.
  • 🤝 Respect Everyone: Abusive, threatening or disruptive behavior toward players or organizers is not allowed.
  • 📢 Official Announcements: Important updates will be communicated through official TSBGMI channels.
  • ⚠️ Disqualification: Cheating or serious rule violations can result in disqualification.
  • 📋 Organizer Decision: Organizer decisions regarding rule violations are final.
  • 📲 Match Information: Players are responsible for checking the official communication channel before every match.
  • ⏳ Late Entry: Players who fail to join the room on time may miss the match.
  • 🔐 Account Responsibility: Players are responsible for their own BGMI accounts, devices and internet connection.
  • 🛡️ Fair Play: All players must maintain fair play and good sportsmanship throughout the tournament.

You must agree to the rules before continuing.
